Alfa-Metal Academy has agreements with high-reputation companies from various countries in Europe and worldwide.

Since 2008, Alfa-Metal has been a leading training center for personnel from major security companies in Europe. The center offers a variety of training programs, such as bodyguards, presidential security,  Incasso, Close Protection, Defensive Driving, Private Military Contractors and much more.

The academy has trained more than 7000 professionals from various sectors, including embassy security, presidential security, private military contractors and companies.

The Academy created and organizes the International Commando Challenge competition, which is held in Gabrovo, Bulgaria, and attracts the most elite structures from the Ministry of Interior, Ministry of Defense and Navy of the Republic of Bulgaria, as well as foreign structures from these departments.

Alfa-Metal Provides:

  • Training, which is carried out in IBTA Alfa-Metal, meets all European and World requirements and standards.
  • Our own base is specially designed so that all kinds of training can be carried out on its territory
  • Training programs for security, law enforcement and military professionals as well as civilians
  • Specialized training using the unification of different methodical systems
  • Training and refreshment training of operators and employees, working in the sphere of protection and security on different kinds of specialties:
    • Static Site Security / Physical Protection of Sites
    • Cash and Valuables in Transit /Bank Security and Collection Activity
    • Bodyguard training
    • Close protection CPO
    • Defensive driving
    • Private Military Contractor PMC
    • CQB
    • Shooting
    • Tactical shooting
    • Maritime Security
    • Diver Preparation – CMAS 1*, 2* and 3*
    • Special VIP Security Driver Training
    • Hostile Environment High Risk Zones training
    • Shooting Instructor training
    • Mobile Patrol teams training
    • Drone operator
  • Training of security personnel from different specialized units of the governmental structures
  • Installation and maintenance of security systems
  • Installation and maintenance of CCTV and fire alarms
  • Installation and maintenance of GPS systems
  • Training and refreshment in shooting (using short and long rifled firearms, defensive shooting) in IPSC methodic and other.
  • Hand – to- hand combat – unique defense system used worldwide by elite special units.
  • First Aid / Tactical Medicine /MIRA /FPOS
  • Tactical Vehicle Driving
  • Firearms Safety usage
  • Personal safety in everyday life and in Hostile Environment areas
  • Legal preparing and practical advices
  • Specialized Psychological Selection

 

NATO & UN Approved Service Provider

Alpha Metal is an approved service provider for NATO (#015BU) and the United Nations (UNGM#820655), delivering training and specialized services in accordance with international operational, security and compliance requirements. Alpha Metal is not part of NATO or the United Nations. Approval refers to supplier/vendor status in accordance with applicable procurement and service frameworks.

This status confirms Alpha Metal’s ability to operate within multinational frameworks and to meet the stringent standards applied by international defense and security organizations.

Since the beginning of 2025, Alfa Metal has been certified by the Civil Aviation Administration as an Aviation Training Center, with certificate No. TS 036.

With us you can take the following courses:

  • FPV drone training course – remote control pilot
  • DJI drone training course – remote control pilot
